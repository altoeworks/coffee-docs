The ultimate French press technique — James Hoffmann

Brew Methods Attribution: James Hoffmann Updated on:

Source: YouTube

Metadata

Ratio
60 g/L
Dose
30 g
Water
500 ml
Grind
medium
Temp
freshly boiled, clean, soft water

Steps

  1. Grind 30 g of coffee at a medium grind.
  2. Put coffee into the French press first, then add 500 ml freshly boiled, clean, soft water.
  3. Mix and make sure no dry coffee remains.
  4. Steep for 4:00.
  5. Mix a bit, then remove all the floaty bits and foam.
  6. Leave for at least 5:00 more minutes.
  7. Put on the mesh filter and press it until it barely touches the surface (not completely down!).
  8. Carefully pour out the coffee without disturbing the bed at the bottom.

  9. Enjoy!
