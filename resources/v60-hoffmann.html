The Ultimate V60 Technique — James Hoffmann

Brew Methods Attribution: James Hoffmann Updated on:

Source: YouTube

Metadata

Ratio
60 g/L
Dose
30 g
Water
500 g total
Grind
medium-fine
Temp
higher temps help (esp. lighter roasts)

Steps

  1. Grind 30 g of coffee.
  2. Rinse paper filter with water just off the boil (removes paper taste; preheats brewer).
  3. Add grounds to V60; create a well in the middle (even bloom saturation).
  4. Start timer (t = 0:00).
  5. Bloom: add 2× dose = 60 g water (avoid >3×); swirl to mix evenly.
  6. Bloom time: up to 0:45.
  7. Pour 1: aim for 60% total = 300 g by 1:15 (add ~240 g in ~30 s ≈ 8 g/s).
  8. Pour 2: aim for 100% total = 500 g by 1:45 (add ~200 g in ~30 s ≈ 6.66 g/s).
  9. Stir 1× clockwise + 1× anticlockwise (knocks grounds off sidewall).
  10. Allow V60 to drain a little; gentle swirl for a flat bed.
  11. Drawdown target: finish by ~3:30.
  12. Enjoy!
